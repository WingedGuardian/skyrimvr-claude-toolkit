### Claude visible channel (automated half of Table V)

A scratch hook returning `systemMessage` (`SKYGUARD-SYSMSG-7781`) and `additionalContext`
(`SKYGUARD-CTX-7782`) on SessionStart and PreToolUse, under `claude -p --output-format stream-json
--verbose --include-hook-events`:

- **PreToolUse `systemMessage`** → a `{"type":"system","subtype":"informational","level":"notice",
  "content":"PreToolUse:Bash says: SKYGUARD-SYSMSG-7781 …"}` event: a user-facing notice. The model did
  **not** see it (asked to quote every SKYGUARD text, it quoted only the two CTX lines).
- **SessionStart `systemMessage`** → **no** informational event; it appears only inside the raw
  `hook_response` event (which exists only because of `--include-hook-events`).
- `additionalContext` reached the model on both events.

So in headless `-p`, a per-call lift notice can reach the user through PreToolUse's `systemMessage`;
the session-start banner cannot rely on SessionStart's `systemMessage`. Whether the interactive TUI
shows either is batch B4 (Table V is decided there, not here).
